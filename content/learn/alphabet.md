# The alphabet

Kazakh is written in a Cyrillic alphabet of **42 letters**: the 33 letters of Russian plus **9 of its own**. A Latin alphabet was approved in 2021 and is being brought in gradually, so you'll meet both. Use the switch at the top of the page to see every Kazakh word on this site in Cyrillic, in Latin, or both.

## The whole alphabet

The 9 letters special to Kazakh are highlighted. Under each letter is its Latin spelling (2021).

Letters marked * appear only in words borrowed from Russian (цирк, чемпион, юрист) and have no Latin letter of their own; Körti writes their sounds.

## The nine Kazakh letters

| Letter | Latin | Sounds like | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ә ә | Ä ä | *a* in “cat” | әке (father) |
| Ғ ғ | Ğ ğ | a soft, throaty *g*, like French *r* | ағаш (tree) |
| Қ қ | Q q | a *k* made far back in the throat | қала (city) |
| Ң ң | Ñ ñ | *ng* in “sing” | жаңа (new) |
| Ө ө | Ö ö | German *ö*: say *e* with rounded lips | өзен (river) |
| Ұ ұ | Ū ū | *u* in “put” | ұл (son) |
| Ү ү | Ü ü | German *ü*: say *i* with rounded lips | үй (house) |
| Һ һ | H h | *h* (rare, in a few loanwords) | гауһар (diamond) |
| І і | I ı | a short *i*, as in “bit” | іні (younger brother) |

## Letters that look familiar but aren't

- **Ы ы** (Latin *Y y*) is a short vowel made at the back of the mouth, a bit like the *e* in “roses”: қыз (girl).
- **У у** is *u* after a consonant but *w* after a vowel: тау (mountain) sounds like *taw*.
- **И и** is *i* or *iy*; **Й й** is *y* as in “yes”. In Latin both are written *İ i*.
- **Е е** at the start of a word sounds *ye*: ертең (tomorrow).

## Vowels come in two families

The vowels split into **back** vowels (а, о, ұ, ы) and **front** vowels (ә, ө, ү, і, е). This matters for every ending in the language: that's [vowel harmony](learn:harmony), the next topic.
