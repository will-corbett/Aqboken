# Plurals

The plural ending is **-лар/-лер**, and it has six shapes. The vowel follows [vowel harmony](learn:harmony); the first consonant follows the word's last sound.

::forms rule plural

## Examples

::forms plural бала әке тау үй қол адам қыз кітап мектеп дос

## When not to use the plural

- After a number: **екі кітап** (two books), **бес бала** (five children). The number already says there's more than one.
- After **көп** (many) and **бірнеше** (several): **көп адам** (many people).

## Endings come one after another

Kazakh piles endings up, always in the same order: word + plural + possessive + case. So “in the books” is кітап + тар + да = **кітаптарда**. Each ending follows the sounds of the one before it.

::drill plural
