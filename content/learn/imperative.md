# Requests and commands

The bare stem is a command to a friend: **Бар!** (Go!), **Кел!** (Come!), **Оқы!** (Read!). Other endings make it polite, plural, or a suggestion.

::forms imperative бару келу оқу айту

## Which one to use

- To a stranger, someone older, a customer: the polite **-(ы)ңыз**: Кіріңіз! (Come in!) Отырыңыз! (Sit down!)
- To several people: **-(ы)ңдар**, or the polite **-(ы)ңыздар**.
- “Let's …”: **-айық/-ейік**: Барайық! (Let's go!)
- “Let me …”: **-айын/-ейін**: Айтайын. (Let me tell you.)
- “Let him/her …”: **-сын/-сін**: Келсін. (Let him come.)

The polite form is the safest choice when you're not sure.

## Don't!

Put the negative **-ма/-ме** (or -ба/-бе, -па/-пе) on the stem: Барма! (Don't go!) Айтпаңыз! (Please don't say it.)

::drill imperative
