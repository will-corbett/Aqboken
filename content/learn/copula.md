# “I am …”

Kazakh has no word for “am” or “are”. Instead, a **personal ending** goes on the noun or adjective:

- Мен мұғаліммін. I am a teacher.
- Сен студентсің. You are a student.
- Ол дәрігер. He (she) is a doctor.
- Біз қазақпыз. We are Kazakhs.

## The endings

::forms copula мұғалім студент қазақ дәрігер жас

- With **мен** (I) and **біз** (we), the ending's first consonant follows the word's last sound: **-мын** after vowels and most voiced sounds, **-бын** after з/ж, **-пын** after voiceless consonants (студентпін, қазақпын).
- With **сен**, **сіз**, **сендер**, **сіздер** the ending always starts with **с**.
- With **ол** (he, she) and **олар** (they) there's no ending at all.

## You, polite and familiar

Kazakh has two “you”s. **Сен** is for a friend, a child or family; **сіз** is polite, for someone older or someone you don't know. In the plural: **сендер** and **сіздер**. Use сіз when in doubt.

## “I'm not …”

Put **емес** (not) after the word, and the personal ending goes on емес: Мен студент **емеспін**. (I'm not a student.)
