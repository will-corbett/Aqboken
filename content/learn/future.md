# Going to

The [present tense](learn:present) already covers the future: Мен ертең барамын (I'll go tomorrow). To say what you **plan or intend** to do, add **-мақ** to the stem, then the [“I am” ending](learn:copula):

- Мен ертең кітапханаға бармақпын. I'm going to go to the library tomorrow.
- Біз кино көрмекпіз. We're going to watch a film.

::forms intention бару оқу айту келу

## The ending

**-мақ/-мек** after vowels and р, й, у, л; **-бақ/-бек** after м, н, ң, з, ж; **-пақ/-пек** after voiceless consonants (айтпақ, кетпек). It's the same consonant rule as the [question particle](learn:questions) and the negative.

Then the personal ending, which after **қ/к** starts with **п**: бармақпын, бармақсың, бармақ, бармақпыз.

## Not going to

Add **емес** with the ending: Мен бармақ емеспін. (I'm not going to go.)
