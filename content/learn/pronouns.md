# Pronouns in the cases

The pronouns take the same seven cases as nouns, but **мен**, **сен** and **ол** have some forms you just need to learn.

::forms pronouns

## The ones to learn by heart

- **маған, саған, оған**: the dative (to me, to you, to him/her) changes the vowel. Маған кітап бер. (Give me a book.)
- **ол** builds most of its forms on **он-**: оның, оны, онда, онымен; but the ablative is **одан**.
- **менімен, сенімен**: “with me, with you” are built on the genitive.

**сіз**, **біз** and **олар** are regular: they take the same endings as nouns (сізге, бізде, олардан).

::drill pronouns
