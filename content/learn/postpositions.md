# Postpositions

Where English has prepositions before a noun, Kazakh has **postpositions** after it. Each one wants the noun before it in a particular case.

::forms postpositions сабақ

## Examples

- Сабақтан кейін. After the lesson.
- Үйге дейін. As far as the house.
- Досыммен бірге. Together with my friend.
- Мен үшін. For me.
- Қазақстан туралы. About Kazakhstan.

## How to learn them

Learn each postposition with its case, as a pair: **-ға дейін**, **-дан кейін**, **-мен бірге**. Most take the plain form (үшін, туралы, арқылы, сияқты); the rest take the dative, ablative or instrumental.

::drill postpositions
