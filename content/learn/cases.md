# The seven cases

Where English uses little words like *to*, *in* and *from*, Kazakh adds an ending. There are seven cases (септіктер); one is just the plain word.

| Case | Ending | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative (Атау) | – | the plain form | мектеп |
| Genitive (Ілік) | -ның/-дың/-тың | of, ’s | мектептің (of the school) |
| Dative (Барыс) | -ға/-қа | to, towards | мектепке (to school) |
| Accusative (Табыс) | -ны/-ды/-ты | the (definite object) | мектепті (the school) |
| Locative (Жатыс) | -да/-та | in, at, on | мектепте (at school) |
| Ablative (Шығыс) | -дан/-нан/-тан | from | мектептен (from school) |
| Instrumental (Көмектес) | -мен/-бен/-пен | with, by | автобуспен (by bus) |

Each ending has front and back forms (-ға/-ге) and changes its first consonant to suit the word's last sound, just like the plural.

## Where you'll use them

- **Dative** for where you're going: Мен мектепке барамын. (I'm going to school.)
- **Locative** for where something is: Ол үйде. (He's at home.)
- **Ablative** for where you're from: Мен Алматыдан келдім. (I came from Almaty.)
- **Instrumental** for how, or with whom: Біз автобуспен барамыз. (We go by bus.) Досыммен (with my friend).
- **Accusative** only for a *particular* thing: Мен кітап оқимын (I read books / a book) but Мен кітапты оқимын (I'm reading *the* book).
- **Genitive** for an owner, with the possessive on the thing owned: баланың кітабы (the child's book). See [My, your, his](learn:possessive).

## The rules for each ending

::forms rule dat
::forms rule loc
::forms rule abl

The genitive is **-ның** after vowels and м/н/ң, **-дың** after other voiced sounds, **-тың** after voiceless ones. The accusative is **-ны** after vowels, **-ды** after voiced consonants, **-ты** after voiceless ones. The instrumental is always **-мен** except after з/ж (**-бен**) and voiceless consonants (**-пен**); it never changes for harmony.
